Tech check, all teams

Do it before the first pitchA team that finds out its adapter does not fit one minute before its pitch has already lost its first minute. Find out now.

Say

  • Five minutes. Every team connects to the projector once, so no one finds out during their slot.
  • If a team cannot mirror, the backup is a phone video held up, or the video file on the teacher's laptop.

Do

  • Walk around. Watch for private-window tests that fail, because that is the thing to fix this week.

Watch for

  • A team whose URL does not open is not failing. They just found their first fix for the week.
Pitch rounds

The clock

Say

  • Say: 'Start when you are ready. When the timer reaches zero, we stop.' Then press start when the team begins to speak.
  • When the pitch ends, press reset on the pitch timer and start the question timer.

Do

  • For each team: reset both timers, press start on the pitch timer when they begin. Judges fill in their scorecards during the questions.
  • Keep time strictly. If a team finishes early, great, start questions.
  • Take one note per team for yourself: the strongest moment, and one thing that confused you.

Watch for

  • Nervous teams will rush. If one does, say gently after: 'You have the time, slow down'. Do not interrupt them mid-pitch.
Debrief

What we saw

  1. Read one strongest moment per team, aloud
  2. Each team reads its scorecard comments silently
  3. Each team writes its top three fixes on a card
  4. I collect the cards and write the common ones on the board
Look for patternsThe same problems repeat across teams. The common ones are the ones to fix first, because they will hurt every pitch on January 20.

Say

  • Say the strongest moment before anything critical. Teams listen to the critical part better after they have heard the good part.
  • The three fixes are for next six days. Fewer fixes done well beats ten half-done.

Do

  • Collect the cards. After class, type up the common problems and post them in the group.
  • Common ones to look for: pitch too long, problem stated too vaguely, demo started late, URL did not open, a team member silent.

Watch for

  • If one team is clearly stronger, do not make them the model. Name specific things they did that others can copy.
