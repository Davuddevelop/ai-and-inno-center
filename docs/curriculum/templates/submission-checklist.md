# Submission checklist

- [ ] Everything the rules ask for is included (repo, link, video, slides, description).
- [ ] The **live URL** opens in a **private window**, signed out, on a **phone**, on **mobile data**.
- [ ] **Two people** tested the final link separately.
- [ ] README: live URL on line three, who did what, the AI log, what we cut.
- [ ] The repo is public, or access is granted as the rules say.
- [ ] **No secrets** anywhere in the history. (If a key was ever committed, rotate it.)
- [ ] The video plays with the sound off and on.
- [ ] Submitted **at least 30 minutes** before the deadline.
- [ ] A screenshot of the confirmation page, saved.
